# OAG college research ledger

## Status

Baseline primed. No curriculum, knowledge repository, preservation service,
tests, or deployment has been created for this project.

## First research questions

- What does “knowledge for all” require for language, disability, literacy,
  bandwidth, offline access, and different prior knowledge?
- Which open licenses and source/provenance models support copying, teaching,
  translation, remixing, and long-term preservation?
- How can knowledge remain plural and correctable without becoming unusably
  uncertain or controlled by a single authority?
- What preservation architecture provides independent copies, integrity checks,
  export, migration, and recovery after organizational failure?
- How should private, dangerous, personally identifying, or legally restricted
  information be handled without using those categories as a general excuse for
  unnecessary censorship?
- What can OAG contribute, and which parts are only adjacent concepts rather than
  implemented integrations?
- How can agents teach and summarize while preserving sources, uncertainty,
  disagreement, and learner independence?

## Next safe step

Define one synthetic knowledge topic with sources, competing interpretations,
a correction, a translation, and an exported archive. Use it to specify
provenance, access, contribution, review, and restoration behavior before
selecting software or a preservation protocol.
