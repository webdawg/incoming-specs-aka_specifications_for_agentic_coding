# OAG vote research ledger

## Status

Baseline primed. No implementation, external research, tests, or deployment has
been performed for this project.

## First research questions

- What kind of decisions should this support: personal, group, organizational,
  or public—and what authority follows from each?
- Which decisions require anonymous ballots, attributable votes, or both?
- How should people inspect evidence, uncertainty, corrections, and minority views?
- What does meaningful consent require when a person is offline, inactive, or
  subject to social or institutional pressure?
- Which voting/counting methods fit the decision types, and what tradeoffs do they
  create for verifiability, privacy, accessibility, and coercion resistance?
- How can agents assist without becoming hidden persuaders or decision-makers?
- What audit, appeal, recount, and recovery behavior is required?
- Is there an evidenced connection to OAG events, identity, mesh, or the proposed
  location-linked service, or are those only adjacent ideas?

## Next safe step

Define one synthetic decision scenario and compare candidate human-centered
flows without collecting real ballots or selecting an implementation. Record the
human authority boundary, information shown to participants, exact decision rule,
and unresolved risks before examining target code.
