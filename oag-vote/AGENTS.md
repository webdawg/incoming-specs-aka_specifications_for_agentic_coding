# Agent entry point

Read the parent `AGENTS.md`, then this folder's `OPERATOR-INTENT.md`, `README.md`,
`SPEC.md`, and `RESEARCH.md` before contributing.

This is an independent specification project. Keep voting, deliberation,
participation, evidence, and continuation artifacts inside this folder. Do not
apply assumptions from `oag-behavior`, `oag-developer_understanding`, or
`oag-location-to-legacy` without checking their scope.

The system may help humans understand choices and record decisions, but humans
must remain able to decide. Do not silently substitute an agent's judgment for a
human decision, manufacture consent, suppress meaningful alternatives, or use
behavioral knowledge to manipulate participation or outcomes. Preserve the
operator's intent verbatim and distinguish requirements, proposals, observed
behavior, inferences, and unknowns.

Do not implement or deploy anything as part of this baseline. Protect private
information and voting data, use synthetic examples, preserve concurrent work,
and inspect exact diffs before any publication.
